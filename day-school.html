A Day At SIGMA
The Day School Experience

School should be more than moving from one class to another.

Every school day should give a child something new to understand, try and enjoy.

At SIGMA, the Day School experience combines focused academics with activities, sports, creativity and meaningful interaction.

Children learn in the classroom, but they also learn while working in teams, solving problems, playing, performing and building friendships.

A Day At SIGMA

Every part of the day has a purpose.

The school day moves between focus, exploration, movement and connection so children stay engaged throughout it.

01 Begin

Arrive & Connect

Begin the day feeling welcomed, settled and ready to learn.

02 Learn

Academic Focus

Build strong concepts through lessons, questions, activities and discussion.

03 Explore

Practical Learning

Use labs, projects, collaborative tasks and hands-on experiences.

04 Move

Sports & Activity

Build energy, discipline, coordination and teamwork through physical activity.

05 Create

Arts & Expression

Make space for imagination, performance, creativity and individual expression.

06 Reflect

End With Purpose

Leave school with new learning, experiences and something to think about.
