Life Beyond The Classroom
Residential Life At SIGMA

School becomes more than a place children attend.

It becomes a community they learn to be part of.

Residential life gives students more time and space to learn, participate, build friendships and discover independence.

Structured routines, academic support, sports, wellbeing and caring adults help children feel supported while gradually learning to take responsibility for themselves.
