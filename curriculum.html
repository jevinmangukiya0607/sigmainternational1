How Children Learn
Our Curriculum

Learn the subject. Understand the idea.

A good curriculum gives children knowledge. A great learning experience helps them understand it.

At SIGMA, academic learning is built around clear concepts, strong foundations and age-appropriate progression.

Lessons encourage children to ask questions, discuss ideas, investigate problems and apply their learning in meaningful ways.

Core Learning Areas

Building strong academic foundations.

Each subject develops important knowledge while also building different ways of thinking.

01

Languages

Read with understanding, express ideas clearly and communicate with confidence.

02
MA

Mathematics

Develop number sense, logical thinking, reasoning and problem-solving.

03

Observe, question, experiment and understand how the world works.

04

Understand people, places, communities, history and the world we share.

Build responsible technology habits and learn to use digital tools purposefully.

06

Connected Learning

The world does not separate itself into subjects.

Real problems often need more than one kind of knowledge.

Wherever appropriate, learning at SIGMA helps children make connections between subjects.

A project may combine mathematics with science, language with history or technology with creativity.

These connections help children understand why they are learning something, not only what they need to remember.

Learning In Context Ideas become stronger when children can connect them.
From Lesson To Learning

Children learn by being part of the process.

Concepts become easier to understand when children can explore, discuss, test and use them.

01

Experiment

Test ideas and discover what happens through hands-on exploration.

02

Collaborate

Listen to different ideas, communicate clearly and solve problems together.

03

Create

Turn understanding into projects, ideas, presentations and original work.

How Understanding Grows

A simple learning cycle that keeps children involved.

01 Begin

Ask

Start with curiosity, questions and what children already know.

02 Discover

Explore

Read, observe, discuss, investigate and experiment.

03 Build

Understand

Organise ideas and develop clear, accurate concepts.

04 Use

Apply

Use learning in exercises, projects and new situations.

05 Improve

Reflect

Look at progress, feedback and what can be improved.

Assessment

Assessment should help learning move forward.

A score tells us something. It does not tell us everything.

Children need opportunities to demonstrate what they understand in different ways.

01

Classroom observation and participation

02

Written work and academic assessments

03

Projects, activities and practical learning

04

Feedback, reflection and improvement
