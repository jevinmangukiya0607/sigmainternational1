About SIGMA

A school built for children who are becoming.

Sigma Horizon School brings academics, life skills, sports, creativity and care together so children can grow with confidence, curiosity and character.

Discover SIGMA ↓
Who We Are

Learning should feel alive.

SIGMA is a future-focused learning community where children are encouraged to ask, explore, try, understand and try again.

We believe school should do more than prepare a child for an examination. It should help children understand themselves, discover their strengths and learn how to work confidently with others.

Academics at SIGMA are complemented by sports, creativity, life skills and meaningful experiences that help children grow beyond the classroom.

The SIGMA Experience Learning happens everywhere.
