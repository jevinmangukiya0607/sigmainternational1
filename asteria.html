ASTERIA

Learning for the mind, heart and life.

ASTERIA brings intellectual, emotional and social growth together so children learn not only what to know, but how to think, feel, connect and act.

A Different Way To Learn
What Is ASTERIA?

Because knowing the answer is only one part of learning.

Children also need to understand themselves, work with others and use what they know.

ASTERIA complements academic learning by paying attention to the different ways a child thinks, feels, communicates and responds to the world.

It connects knowledge with curiosity, relationships, responsibility and real-life experiences.

Whole-Child Development

Three dimensions. One growing child.

ASTERIA looks beyond academic ability alone and supports intellectual, emotional and social growth.

How ASTERIA Works

Learning moves from listening to doing.

Children understand more when they take an active role in learning.

Questions, conversations, projects, reflection and practical experiences allow students to connect ideas with the world around them.

01
Observe

Notice

Look closely, listen and become curious about something.

02

03

Test ideas through activities, projects and practical work.

04

Consider what worked, what changed and what was learned.

05
Apply

Carry learning into a new problem, situation or experience.

CBSE + ASTERIA

Strong academics. A wider learning experience.

ASTERIA does not replace academic foundations. It strengthens the way children experience them.

The CBSE curriculum provides academic structure, while the wider SIGMA learning approach creates opportunities to question, experience, communicate and apply learning.
