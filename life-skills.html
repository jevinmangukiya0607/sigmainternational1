Learning Beyond Books
Why Life Skills Matter

Knowing things is important. Knowing how to handle life matters too.

Children grow every time they make a choice, solve a problem or take responsibility.

Life skills develop through everyday experiences — speaking with others, working in teams, managing routines, solving disagreements and learning from mistakes.

At SIGMA, these moments are part of education, not separate from it.

Decision-Making

Not every problem comes with four options.

Real life asks children to think before they choose.

Learning to make decisions means considering options, consequences, other people and what feels responsible.

01
Pause

What is happening?

Understand the situation before reacting.

02
Think

What can I do?

Consider different choices and possible solutions.

03
Consider

What happens next?

Think about consequences for yourself and others.

04
Choose

What is the better choice?

Make a thoughtful decision and take responsibility for it.
