Book a Campus Visit

The best answers are the ones you see.

Pick a day that suits your family. We will call to confirm and plan the visit around what matters most to you.

  1. 01

    Meet the admissions team

    A relaxed conversation about your child, their interests and what you are looking for.

  2. 02

    Walk through the campus

    See classrooms, labs, the library, sports spaces and the dining hall in a normal school day.

  3. 03

    See residential life

    Considering boarding? Visit the houses and meet the people who look after children every day.

  4. 04

    Ask us anything

    Curriculum, fees, transport, scholarships. Leave with clear answers and the next steps.
