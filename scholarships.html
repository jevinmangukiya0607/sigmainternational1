Scholarship Support

Opportunity should help potential move forward.

Every child brings different strengths, experiences and possibilities to school.

Scholarship opportunities, where available, can help recognise students and support families exploring admission at SIGMA.

Because scholarship criteria can change by academic year, grade and programme, families should confirm current information directly with the admissions team.

Why Scholarships Matter

Recognising effort, potential and achievement.

Scholarship support can create another pathway for families to explore a SIGMA education.

01
P
Potential

Encourage Growth

Recognising students who demonstrate the potential to keep learning and developing.

02
E
Effort

Value Commitment

Celebrating consistent effort, discipline and a willingness to improve.

03
A
Achievement

Recognise Strengths

Giving families a way to share meaningful academic or other student achievements.

What To Share With Us

Help us understand your child's strengths.

When asking about scholarships, families can share relevant student achievements and background.

The admissions team can then explain whether any current scholarship opportunity applies and what further information may be required.

01
Academics

Academic Achievement

Recent academic records can help the admissions team understand the student's learning journey.

02
Sports

Sporting Achievement

Families may share relevant participation, achievements or recognition in sporting activities.

03
Co-Curricular

Activities & Interests

Relevant achievements in creative, cultural or co-curricular areas may also be shared.

04
Student Story

Effort & Development

Families can also discuss circumstances, progress or other information relevant to their enquiry.

How To Explore A Scholarship

Start with a conversation.

Scholarship information should be confirmed before families make assumptions about eligibility or support.

01 Enquire

Contact Admissions

Tell us your child's grade and that you would like to understand current scholarship opportunities.

02 Share

Provide Information

Share any relevant academic, sporting or co-curricular information requested.

03 Understand

Review Eligibility

The admissions team can explain the currently applicable eligibility and process.

04 Continue

Proceed With Admission

Complete the applicable admission steps and any scholarship-related requirements communicated by the school.

Before You Enquire

A little preparation makes the conversation easier.

You do not need to guess what qualifies. Simply keep useful student information available and let the admissions team guide you.

01
Student's Grade

The grade for which admission is being explored.

02
Recent Academic Information

School records or results where requested.

03
Achievement Details

Relevant certificates, participation or recognition, if applicable.

04
Your Questions

Ask clearly about eligibility, process and current availability.

*
Important To Know

Scholarship details may change.

Availability, eligibility, evaluation requirements and scholarship benefits should always be confirmed with SIGMA directly.

Please do not rely on assumptions, older information or unofficial figures when planning an application.

Families should continue to understand the school, programme and admission process as a whole.

Explore SIGMA, speak with admissions, understand Day or Residential School options and review current scholarship information before proceeding.
