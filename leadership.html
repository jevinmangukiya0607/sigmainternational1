Our Leadership

Great schools begin with people who care deeply about education.

At SIGMA, leadership is about more than managing a school. It is about shaping an environment where children can learn, grow and become confident people.

Our school brings together the educational legacy of Sigma University and the school-building expertise of Bellwether International. Together, they guide the academic, cultural and developmental direction of Sigma Horizon School.
