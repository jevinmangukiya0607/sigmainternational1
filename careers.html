Work At SIGMA

A good school needs good people who care.

Teaching is important. But so are the people, systems and relationships surrounding every classroom.

At SIGMA, educators and professionals work as one community to create a safe, engaging and ambitious environment for children.

We value people who are curious, responsible, collaborative and willing to keep learning.
