How Admission Works
A Simple Process

One decision. Clear steps.

Choosing a school already involves enough thinking. The admission process should not add confusion.

Begin by learning about SIGMA and speaking with our admissions team. From there, families can visit the school, understand the available options and complete the application.

Our team can guide you through each stage and help answer questions along the way.
