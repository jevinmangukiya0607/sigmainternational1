Welcome to SIGMA

A school children can understand, enjoy and belong to.

Meet SIGMA →

SIGMA is a place where children come to learn, make friends, play, ask questions and discover what they are good at. We combine the CBSE curriculum with sports, creativity, life skills and thoughtful student care.

Learn Understand concepts, ask questions and explore.
Create Explore ideas through art, STEAM and activities.
Grow Develop independence, empathy and responsibility.

Residential Life

More than a place to stay. A place to belong.

When children live away from home, parents want to know one thing first: who will care for them? At SIGMA, residential life is built around care, routine, wellbeing and independence.

Home away from home Learning · Living · Growing

Life beyond the classroom matters too.

Students follow a balanced routine with academics, study support, sports, recreation, meals, friendships and rest.

House Parents Caring adults who guide, supervise and support students.
Study Support Structured evening learning and healthy study habits.
Wellbeing Balanced routines, meals, health support and recreation.
Independence Everyday life helps students become organised, responsible and confident.
