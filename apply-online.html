    What Happens Next

    You apply. We take it from here.

    No long wait and no guesswork. This is how your application moves forward.

    1. 01

      We review

      The admissions team reads your application and checks seat availability for the grade.

    2. 02

      We call you

      Usually within one working day, to answer questions and understand your child better.

    3. 03

      You visit

      Meet the team, see the campus and, for residential families, the boarding houses.

    4. 04

      We confirm

      Documents, fees and joining dates, explained clearly before anything is final.

    Keep These Handy

    Nothing to upload today.

    You don't need any documents to apply online. We'll ask for these later, when your admission is being confirmed.

    • ✓Child's birth certificate
    • ✓Recent passport-size photographs
    • ✓Aadhaar card of the child and parents
    • ✓Last report card (Grade 1 and above)
    • ✓Transfer / leaving certificate from the previous school
    • ✓Address proof
