Academics At SIGMA

Learn to understand. Not memorise.

Strong academics begin when children understand what they learn, ask better questions and know how to use knowledge beyond the classroom.

Explore Learning ↓ View Curriculum →
How We See Learning

Knowledge matters. Knowing what to do with it matters more.

We want children to understand ideas, not simply remember answers.

At SIGMA, strong academic foundations are combined with questions, discussions, experiments, projects and real experiences.

This helps children become confident learners who can think independently, solve problems and connect what they learn to the world around them.

Inside The Classroom

Learning is something children should do.

A classroom becomes powerful when children participate in the learning instead of simply listening to it.

01
Q

Question

Encourage children to ask why, how and what happens if we try something different.

02
E

Explore

Investigate ideas through activities, experiments, discussion and observation.

03

Understand

Build clear concepts and connect new learning with what children already know.

04

Apply

Use knowledge to solve problems, create something and make better decisions.

Education should develop more than academic intelligence.
